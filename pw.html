Clarity for Your Journey

Honest answers to the questions that matter most.

What is PlacidWay and how does it work?

PlacidWay is a global medical tourism platform that helps patients explore treatments, compare clinics, request free quotes, and connect with healthcare providers in different countries. You can search by procedure or destination, review options, and start planning your treatment journey in one place.

Is medical tourism safe?

Medical tourism can be safe when you choose qualified providers, review treatment details carefully, and plan for follow-up care before you travel. It is important to ask about provider credentials, risks, recovery time, communication, and what support is available if complications happen.

How do I choose the best clinic or doctor abroad?

Compare the clinic’s experience, doctor qualifications, patient reviews, treatment focus, and destination options before making a decision. You should also ask what is included in the quote, what aftercare is provided, and who will help if you need support after treatment.

What treatments can I find on PlacidWay?

PlacidWay features a wide range of treatments, including dental care, plastic surgery, fertility treatment, stem cell therapy, weight loss surgery, addiction treatment, cancer treatment and orthopedic procedures. Popular options include dental implants, All-on-4 dental implants, full mouth restoration, tummy tuck, liposuction, IVF, gastric sleeve, and hip or knee replacement.

Can I get dental implants abroad through PlacidWay?

Yes, dental care is one of the main categories on PlacidWay, including dental implants, All-on-4 dental implants, veneers, crowns, dentures, and full mouth restoration.

Can I book cosmetic or plastic surgery abroad through PlacidWay?

Yes, PlacidWay highlights cosmetic and plastic surgery options such as tummy tuck, mommy makeover, Brazilian Butt Lift, breast augmentation, facelift, liposuction, and hair transplant.

Does PlacidWay help with fertility treatment abroad?

Yes, PlacidWay features fertility services such as IVF, IVF with gender selection, egg donation, surrogacy, and tubal ligation reversal.

Can I compare treatment options in different countries?

Yes, PlacidWay is built to help patients compare providers and treatment options across multiple destinations. The company says it has a network of more than 2,000 clinics in over 60 countries and 250 cities.

How do I get a medical quote from PlacidWay?

You can start by requesting a free quote through the platform and sharing your treatment needs. From there, you can review provider options, compare destinations, and evaluate the details of your care plan.

Do I need my medical records before treatment abroad?

Yes, sharing your medical history and relevant records helps providers evaluate your case more accurately before treatment. It is also a good idea to take your records with you and arrange follow-up care when you return home.

Why do patients choose PlacidWay?

Patients choose PlacidWay because it offers access to many treatment categories, global destination options, patient reviews, and a long-standing presence in medical tourism since 2007.
